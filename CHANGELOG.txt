  decomposition rules to place smaller legal groups when a large group fails.

  footprints, bed geometry, textures, and the configuration editor preview.

  Reject candidates that disconnect any existing or new bed from site-boundary

  Serialize road holes and isolated pockets; always audit final connectivity.
